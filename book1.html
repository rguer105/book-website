The Hunger Games

Author: Suzanne Collins

The Hunger Games is a dystopian novel about Katniss Everdeen, a teenager who volunteers to participate in a deadly competition called the Hunger Games. The story follows her struggle to survive while challenging the powerful Capitol.

I chose this book because it has an exciting story, interesting characters, and a lot of action.
